The Green Castle

The forests of Kalonia were once home to the Green Castle. Now, however, the land where it once stood has been reclaimed by nature: To an untrained eye it would appear to be no more than an average forest clearing. You know better, though: Any trained mage can tell there's something special about this spot. Tapping into the rich reserves of green mana left here will require you to attune yourself to nature, and to the history of the land. You close your eyes and begin to focus...

Green is the color of tradition and history. The Green Castle contains trivia about Magic's story and lore, as well as in-universe card names and flavor text.
