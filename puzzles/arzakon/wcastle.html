The White Castle

The radiant spires of the White Wizard's castle stand before you, upon the steppes of Thune. Despite its age and abandonment, the castle seems to gleam with untouched purity, certainly the result of lingering wards placed upon it from the era of Arzakon's first siege of Shandalar. With courage in your heart, you take the first step into the castle...

White is the color of community and the common good. To that end, the White castle contains trivia that the other colors would scorn: The outer edges of Magic as a game and a brand. Silver bordered cards, playtest cards, obscure promos and Secret Lair printings, marketing materials, crossovers, tournament history, and more. If it's a part of "everything else", it's here.
