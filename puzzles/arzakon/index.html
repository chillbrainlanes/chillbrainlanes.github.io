The Return of Arzakon!

Eons ago, the archdemon Arzakon layed siege to the plane of Shandalar. A lone heroic planeswalker put a stop to his onslaught, banishing him to the recesses of history. Now, however, Arzakon has begun to gather power, with sightings of the demon and his works being reported by mages at Mystery Booster: Commander Edition draft events across the plane.

With Arzakon's return, Shandalar has been plunged into chaos. The plane's wise sages have convened and devised a new plan to banish Arzakon once more. Lying in ruins across the world are the castles of Arzakon's five mighty wizard generals. Slain at the hands of the heroic planeswalker all those ages ago, these fortresses are still home to rich wells of powerful mana for those willing to brave their perilous traps.

It's now up to you, another heroic vagabond, to tap into the castles' mana in order to cast a spell powerful enough to thwart Arzakon's conquest. The more power you're able to gather, the longer he'll be exiled for. Good luck, planeswalker!

Reeturn of Arzakon is a Magic: The Gathering trivia adventure. Each castle is home to a different themed category of trivia questions, ranging from the entirety of the game's history.

Each castle is divided into three chambers of different difficulties, each with 30 questions. The easiest questions are worth 20 points, medium 40, and the hardest 60. In each question you'll be given a number of characters, along with spaces and punctuation: The answer to the question will fit into the provided space. In addition, each question has a hint: When a hint is revealed, answering the question correctly will award you with half points. You'll only get one cance to submit your answer for each question, so think carefully!

Progress is saved in your browser, so don't hesitate to tackle this challenge in multiple sessions. At any time, if you've answered every question you can, you can confront Arzakon to earn your final score. Once you confront Arzakon, the game is over and you won't be able to answer any more questions.

Good luck, and have fun!
