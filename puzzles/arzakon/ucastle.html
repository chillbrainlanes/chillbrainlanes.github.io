The Blue Castle

Eons of changing terrain have submerged the castle of the Blue Wizard deep into the seas surrounding Eos Isle. While the waves have claimed the structure, the mana within is still as rich as ever. With a spell of breath cast upon your lungs, you plunge into the sea in search of the untold treasures of blue mana.

Blue is the color of practical knowledge. The riddles of the Blue Wizard's castle are concerned with typical printings of Vintage-legal, in-universe cards. This is mostly concerned with game mechanics, such as oracle text and game rules, but other trivia associated with these cards that do not fall into the domains of the other castles may also be included.
