The Red Castle

The Red Castle sits atop Mount Valkas, the tallest peak on the plane of Shandalar. Once home to the Red Wizard, known as the Dragon Master, dragons still roost upon its ramparts. The vast deposits of red mana within the castle are only rivaled by its spectacular hordes of treasure. In spite of the castle's many temptations, you stay your course, determined to harvest the necessary mana to banish Arzakon.

Red is the color of fiery passion and creativity: The Red Castle's questions all concern card art.
